## 1. What Real Students Love (Core Strengths)
* **Campus & Tech Infrastructure:** Massive permanent green campus in Ashulia, golf course, smart classrooms, coding clubs, robotics labs.
* **Extracurricular & Hackathon Ecosystem:** Strong support for regional hackathons, startup pitching, and incubation centers.
* **Middle-tier Pricing:** More affordable compared to NSU / BRAC with high infrastructure availability.

## 2. What Real Students Criticize / Hate (Gaps to Solve)
1. **Commute / Distance Agony:** Ashulia is far from central Dhaka; daily 3-4 hour traffic burns out students.
   * *The Fix in our Clone:* Add an interactive **Campus Shuttle Tracker & Route Finder** and integrated residential hostel booking flow.
2. **Batch Crowd & Mentorship Access:** Large student batches make direct faculty mentorship difficult.
   * *The Fix in our Clone:* Dedicated **Faculty Office-Hours Booking & Mentorship Portal** right in the portal.
3. **ERP Complexity & Information Fragmentation:** Multiple older portals (Student portal, Notice board, payment gateway) create confusion.
   * *The Fix in our Clone:* Single Unified Dashboard combining fees, notices, grades, and shuttle passes.

## 3. Positioning Angle for our Rebrand
* **Brand Name Angle:** **Apex International University (AIU)** or **Verdant Institute of Technology (VIT)**.
* **Value Proposition:** *"World-Class Green Campus Infrastructure + Hybrid Commute Intelligence & 1-on-1 Mentorship Guarantee."*
